Customer Account Statement — Portal & PDF

Send your customers a clear, professional account statement showing their pending invoices, payments applied, and outstanding balance — directly from the partner form, by email with the PDF attached, or let customers check it themselves in the self-service portal.

🖨 Print PDF

Generate a branded PDF statement for any date range in one click from the partner form.

✉ Send by Email

Attach the PDF automatically and open the compose dialog pre-filled with the customer's address.

🌐 Customer Portal

Customers log in to /my/statement and view or download their own statement at any time.

⚠ Overdue Highlight

Overdue lines are highlighted in red on both the PDF and the portal page.

How it works

  1. Open any customer (res.partner) and click the Account Statement smart button.
  2. Choose the date range (leave "From" empty for full history).
  3. Click Print PDF or Send by Email.
  4. Customers with portal access can also visit /my/statement to see and download their statement.

What the statement shows

  • Opening balance (entries before the selected start date)
  • All posted invoices, credit notes, and payments in chronological order
  • Running balance column with colour-coded overdue dates
  • Summary box: Total Invoiced · Total Paid · Amount Due

Compatibility

Odoo 18.0 Community Enterprise Requires: Accounting, Portal

Technical notes

  • PDF rendering requires wkhtmltopdf installed on the server (standard Odoo requirement).
  • All amounts are displayed in the company currency.
  • Multi-company aware: each wizard is scoped to a single company.
  • No new database tables are created — uses a transient model.
