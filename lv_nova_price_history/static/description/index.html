Customer Price History on Quotations

Never guess what you charged a customer last time. As soon as you add a product to a quotation, this module silently checks every confirmed sale order for that customer and shows you the last price charged — right on the line, with one click to see the full history.

🕓 One-click history

A small clock icon appears next to the unit price whenever history exists. Click it to see every past order, date and price.

📊 Optional inline columns

Enable "Last Price", "Last Sold" and "Δ %" columns from the list's gear icon to see everything without clicking.

⚖ Price variation %

Instantly see if the current quote is higher or lower than the last confirmed price — color-coded red/green.

👨‍👩‍👧 Company-wide contacts

History is matched at the commercial entity level, so it works across different contacts of the same company.

How it works

  1. Open any quotation and select a customer.
  2. Add a product line — if that customer has bought it before (confirmed order), a small history icon appears next to the price.
  3. Click the icon to see every past order with date, quantity and price.
  4. Optionally enable the "Last Price" / "Last Sold" / "Δ %" columns for an always-visible view.

Compatibility

Odoo 18.0 Community Enterprise Requires: Sales

Technical notes

  • No new database tables — works on existing confirmed sale order lines.
  • Multi-company aware: history is scoped to the order's company.
  • Read-only popup: clicking history never lets you edit a past order.
