Withholding Taxes (LATAM) — Customer & Vendor

Built for countries where customers and companies act as withholding agents (Ecuador, Colombia, Peru, and similar regimes). Register income and VAT withholding lines on any customer invoice or vendor bill, let the module create the matching accounting entry automatically, and print a withholding certificate — all without leaving the document.

⚙ Configurable tax types

Define as many withholding tax types as you need: income tax, VAT, by percentage and target account.

🧾 One-click journal entry

Generates and posts the accounting entry automatically, reconciling it against the original invoice or bill.

📄 Withholding certificate PDF

Print a clean certificate with a sequential number for your records or to hand to the counterparty.

🔄 Sales & purchases

Works both ways: when your customer withholds from you, and when you withhold from a vendor.

How it works

  1. Configure your withholding tax types under Accounting → Configuration → Withholding Taxes.
  2. On a posted customer invoice or vendor bill, open the Withholding tab and add one or more lines.
  3. Click Create Withholding Entry — the module posts the journal entry and reconciles it automatically.
  4. Click Print Withholding Certificate to generate the PDF with a sequential certificate number.

Compatibility

Odoo 18.0 Community Enterprise Requires: Accounting / Invoicing

Technical notes

  • Uses a dedicated miscellaneous journal — create one under Accounting if your company doesn't have one yet.
  • The generated entry is automatically reconciled against the invoice's receivable/payable line.
  • Certificate numbering uses a standard Odoo sequence, configurable per company.
  • This module does not file taxes automatically — it produces the bookkeeping entry and certificate; verify the legal format required by your local authority.
